# Art and sound

## Visual language

MENDWIND is an original hand-painted pastoral fantasy: a timber repair shop, vast summer clouds, modest wind-powered machinery and objects with a past. Its palette combines warm paper `#f5f1df`, deep leaf green `#2e4b3c`, muted rust `#af5c3c`, straw gold and distant blue-green hills. Typography pairs Cormorant Garamond with DM Sans; Chinese text uses Noto Serif SC.

The interface uses stitched-paper notes, small illustrated tools, exact material counts and deliberate empty space. Avoid neon, heavy glass panels, harsh electronic sound, glossy plastic and busy dashboard layouts. The illustrated setting is decorative; readable controls and game records remain HTML.

Wind seeds and slow cloud movement establish atmosphere. Pointer parallax stays below ten pixels. A gust briefly accelerates the seeds. Mended objects receive stamps; the completed valley brightens and a letter takes flight. Reduced-motion mode disables the decorative motion.

## Illustration brief

The hero at `public/assets/wind-valley.webp` was created with the built-in ImageGen tool, then encoded as WebP for the website. The original image is 1672 × 941 pixels. The production prompt was:

> Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: spectacular wide hero background illustration for an original web game MENDWIND, no typography. Create a breathtaking panoramic hand-painted Japanese animated fantasy film background: a charming timber-and-plaster repair workshop with a terracotta roof and huge cream cloth windmill on a grassy floating hill above a lush emerald valley, enormous luminous cumulus clouds filling a turquoise summer sky, little weathered copper pipes, a clothesline of tiny colorful cloth patches, wooden footbridge curving toward distant miniature hamlets, wild daisies, drifting swallows, an elegant little wood-and-canvas postal glider moored beside the workshop. Rich gouache and transparent watercolor texture, exquisite illustrated fine architectural details, warm nostalgic handmade atmosphere, deep scenic layers, gentle wind and dappled sunlight, captivating airy sense of adventure. Cinematic landscape aspect 16:9 or wider, workshop dominates right two thirds, left third a beautiful atmospheric luminous sky valley with slightly quieter low-contrast space for HTML title overlay. Art-directed blue-green, cream, warm amber and vermilion palette. Not photorealistic, not 3D render, no UI, no cards, no text, no letters, no watermarks. Original architecture and vehicles, no existing franchise characters.

The windmill mark and all interface object/material illustrations are original code-native vector drawings. The submission PNG uses an opaque paper background to avoid exposing a host application's fallback color at its corners.

## Original score

`public/audio/windworkshop.mp3` is a 105.85-second original 6/8 composition in D major. Felt-piano arpeggios carry a simple original melody, followed by a breathy flute statement, soft strings and sparse celesta. The recording uses synthesized instruments, no external samples and no borrowed soundtrack melody.

Stereo, 44.1 kHz, 128 kb/s. Measured mean level: −18.8 dBFS; decoded maximum: −5.4 dBFS. Frontend volume is 0.4. Two-second opening and four-second closing fades soften the loop boundary. Playback starts enabled and respects browser autoplay restrictions; a manual mute remains available.
