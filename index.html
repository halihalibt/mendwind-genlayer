MWIND REPAIR
SOCIETY
01 /

Say it in your own words.

In live play, explain what you intend to repair. GenLayer’s leader and validators interpret the original plan independently and must agree on every operation. Vague or unsupported plans spend no materials.

02 /

Keep what you can. Use what you have.

Each repair has a fixed recipe. The contract checks the entire plan before spending anything. Dismantling returns fixed reclaimed parts, permanently retires the object, and can happen only once.

03 /

A story, not an engineering manual.

All objects and materials are fictional. There are no payments, tokens or prizes. Restore all three objects within twelve attempts to bring the valley back. Real consensus receipts are kept separate from practice sketches.
